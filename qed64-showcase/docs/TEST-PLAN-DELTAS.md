## 3. Other deviations and notes

- **The golden generator is `lean/goldens/lsp-golden.mjs`, not per-package `*.probe.lean`.** This is a stage A deviation, kept. The driver now also does `--click-all`, `--env w7|w8` and `codeActions`.
- **BUILD-PLAN E3 step 9** says to freeze the wasm results into `lean/expect/<pkg>.json`. That path holds the native goldens. Write the wasm results elsewhere (e.g. `out/e3/<pkg>.json`) and compare them on the env-independent fields.
- **Example size**: every example is at most 60 lines. chart-kit 60, interval-inspector 56, tree-scope 48, expr-xray 42, graph-scope 38, simp-lens 37, dist-lens 35, hasse-view 34.
- **The closure-env sensitivity is unchanged** (information only). Only expr-xray and simp-lens are env-sensitive. simp-lens post-click files 1, 3 and 4 fail the closure CLI gate by design (`add_zero` is a Mathlib name), and its closure hover is `Nat`. In the closure env the simp-lens code actions still equal the closure-env Try-this edits (6/6).
- **Timings are native only.** Click re-elaboration is about 0.5–0.9 s natively. The wasm cost of the graph-scope/hasse-view gates and of the dist-lens `pmf_num` clicks is still for the UX lane to measure (300 s DistLens budget).
